# How do I use Hybrid recording?

In Settings, choose Hybrid under Trigger. Hold the hotkey for at least 300 ms,
then release to finish. For hands-free recording, tap briefly and press again
within 300 ms of releasing. Release and keep speaking; press again to finish.
Both taps share one recording. The pill looks the same as held recording.

A short single tap finishes after the 300 ms second-tap window. Key repeat does
not count as a second tap. The selected mode also applies to note and transform
shortcuts. Hold and Toggle keep their existing behavior.

Changing shortcuts, capturing a new shortcut, locking the screen, or sleeping
finishes an active Hybrid take. Canceling a take clears its pending gesture.
